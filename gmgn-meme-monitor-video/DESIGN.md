## Style Prompt

Dark premium desktop trading utility. The video should feel like a compact Bloomberg or TradingView terminal made for meme coin monitoring: glassy black panels, precise neon green/red data feedback, sharp numeric hierarchy, dense but breathable layout, and fast warning motion. The tone is professional, urgent, and clean rather than playful.

## Colors

- Background: `#030607`
- Panel Glass: `#091113`
- Stroke: `#1f3430`
- Primary Green: `#35da8f`
- Alert Red: `#ff4d5f`
- Text Primary: `#f2fff9`
- Text Muted: `#89a7a0`

## Typography

- Display and Chinese UI: `Noto Sans JP`
- Financial numbers: `JetBrains Mono`
- Use tabular numbers for market cap, price, percentage, and timestamps.

## What NOT to Do

- No bright marketing gradients or playful illustrations.
- No oversized empty hero layout; every scene should feel like a real desktop tool.
- No crowded tiny unreadable dashboards.
- No light theme panels.
- No generic stock trading screenshots.
